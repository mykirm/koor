The corpus is built from **your own real self-tracked data** plus a small, clearly-labeled donor extension. The only thing left to write is the **thought** for each of the 23 rows in `data/thoughts.authoring.json`. Claude will not write the narratives.

## What's real vs borrowed (the honesty contract)

| Field | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `thought`, `resolved_outcome`, `days_to_resolve` | **You** — write these | The only blanks. One real reflection per row. |
| `date`, `phase`, `sleep_hours` (Tier 1, 13 rows) | **You** — your tracker + sleep app | Real, self-tracked May 2026. Pre-filled. |
| `phase`, `sleep_hours` (Tier 2, 10 rows) | **Donor** — mcPHASES participant 24's real nights | Real co-measured donor sleep, phase-matched; date is a phase-relative label. Disclosed as donor's, not yours. |
| `hrv_ms` | **Dropped** | We tested borrowing HRV from a matched donor; a top-k sensitivity check showed it was donor-trait-dominated, not phase-driven (see `data/hrv-donor-analysis.md`). Koor now grounds on **phase + sleep + retrieved past thoughts** only. |

## How retrieval will behave

- **late_luteal — 8 real days:** your strongest cluster → low-novelty "name the pattern" grounding. The headline contrast lives here.
- **follicular / ovulatory / menstrual — 5 each:** smaller clusters (3 real + 2 donor / 1 real + 4 donor / 1 real + 4 donor).

Write what was actually going through your head — don't engineer the thoughts to fit anything.

## How to fill it in

Fill the blank `thought` / `resolved_outcome` / `days_to_resolve` for all 23 rows in `data/thoughts.authoring.json`. `resolved_outcome` = what actually happened in hindsight (brief, or `""` if still open); `days_to_resolve` = integer days or `null`. If you can't recall a real thought for a Tier-2 slot, delete that row rather than invent one. When done, tell me — I'll validate against `CorpusSchema`, write `thoughts.json` + provenance, re-derive the eval scenarios from your actual lanes, and run the eval.

**I won't write your thoughts, and I won't run the eval on placeholder content.**
